Hybrid Scoring Engine

Text Match (35%) RapidFuzz token-sort ratio combined with TF-IDF cosine similarity on normalized text with controlled synonym mapping (pothole, streetlight, garbage, drain).

Spatial Radius (40%) Geographic distance score mapping Haversine distance (1.0 for ≤30m, 0.75 for ≤150m, 0.50 for ≤250m).

Category & Proximity (25%) Category match (15%), temporal date proximity (5%), and image path matching (5%).

Resolution-Event Linkage

Workflow Chain Multiple Complaints → Single Underlying Issue → Resolution Event History.

Status Tracking Supports NEW, POSSIBLE_DUPLICATE, CONFIRMED_DUPLICATE, UNDERLYING_ISSUE_CREATED, RESOLVED, UNRESOLVED, and INVALID_DATA.

Scalability Candidate generation strategy reduces pairwise comparisons from O(N2) to O(Ncat · K), yielding a 99.88% search space reduction.
